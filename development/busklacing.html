For the busk, a piece of wood that goes in the front of the stays to give the fabric some shaping, a classic costuming trick is to cut down a wooden school ruler and use that. So, I bought a cheap ruler from Officeworks, took it to my work at The Creator Space, and cut and sanded it into the size and shape that I needed. For the lacing, I braided together six pieces of mercerized cotton embroidery thread. This is a type of yarn that has been treated in an alkaline or acidic bath in order to increase the strength and shine.
