Crazy 8s was the very first activity that we undertook to begin developing our response to the Copy of a Copy Brief. On a sheet of paper, you have 8 minutes to sketch 8 ideas. I chose to centre the idea of ritual and tradition as my entry point for this exercise. It pushed my thoughts and visual expression of my ideas in different directions - towards conspiracy theories about satanic 'rituals' and the ritual of making a covenant/agreement. The idea that I eventually ended up pursuing - that of processing and replication as a the thing that produces art - is present in my crazy 8s.
