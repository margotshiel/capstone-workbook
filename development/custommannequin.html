I wanted to try David's idea of manipulating the type through the use of my own body as a three dimensional form, but first wanted to try and avoid having to use my actual body to do that! I turned to a well known technique for making a custom mannequin mold, with the hopes that I could somehow project my text onto this instead of myself. This process is very straight forward; you wrap yourself in glad wrap, trying not to compress the body too much, or change its shape. Then you cover that in a layer of tape, and cut this cast off of the body. This cast can then be cut up to produce a flat pattern, which you make up using fabric and stuff with stuffing. This produces a custom mannequin that is generally faithful enough that draping and pinning pieces becomes infinitely easier. However, for my purposes, I do feel like it altered my body too much, and that to project onto this form, would have kind of nothing to do with me.
