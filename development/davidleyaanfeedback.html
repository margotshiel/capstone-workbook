In the week 2 class, I explained my interests and direction to David and Leyaan, and discussed my research into Joseph Campbell, historical fashion and its occasional controversy, and my desire to create a 1810s/regency style gown with flowing, layered fabric. We crystalised my idea to basically be: 1 story, 3 formats. Leyaan suggested that I could display the garment portion using a live model in a clear box, like a live mannequin. David and I agreed that there needed to be some instance of movement with the way that the textile was displayed. By this point, I had also decided that returning to cyanotypes as a method for applying text to fabric would be the process that I followed.
