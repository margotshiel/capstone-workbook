For each of the three dresses, I was really guided by the fabric itself, and by looking at how the print had come out. The first decision that this helped me make, was the decision to have three dresses, instead of combining all three pieces of fabric into one garment. This was also informed by my research into the Rainbow Magic fairy books, and the desire to have three similar by slightly different characters/guises/chapters in my hero's journey. The cyanotype piece was the first dress that I draped, which I did just be playing around with wrapping myself in the fabric while wearing the shaping under garment of the stays. I did this over a couple of days, really taking my time and just letting myself be guided by how the fabric was moving.
