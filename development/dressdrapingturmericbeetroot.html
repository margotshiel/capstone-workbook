My process for draping the beetroot and turmeric dresses was very similar to the cyanotype one. Each fabric had a slightly different weight, drape and feel, which meant that they did each behave differently on my body. I generally began by inserting a zipper down the back of the dress and creating, in essence, a tube, with extra fabric around the neck line that I would manipulate into sleeves and an almost bodice. Obviously, none of this is historically accurate in any way, shape, or form, but it does feel true to the feeling of lightness that I get from looking at portraits and fashion plates from the 1810s.
