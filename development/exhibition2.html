Sitting in class, in week 12, I did a second rough visualisation of my exhibition set up. I still do not think that I nailed it, but I have introduced some exciting new ideas. I have three mannequins rather than one, as I am considering making three separate dresses with my fabric. I have also included one of David's ideas in my feedback from week 11, which is a cardboard cut out, movie star style, of me in my costume, brandishing my needle aloft. Behind these large forms is a triptych of printed photos of me in the dresses, and beside this is a table with a book and a monitor. The book is covered with fake grass. I like this, but it still does not feel like it is quite yet a single connected idea or piece...
