I decided that I needed to revisit my exhibition set up and consider how to display a book and three pieces of cloth effectively. This was when I began thinking, again, of Cicely Mary Brown's fairy illustrations and fairy gardens filled with wonder. I had already considered encorporating fake turf in my install, but here I took it a step further, using a piece of turf to designate a little magical corner where the fabric is held up by branches. People will be invited to come and sit on the grass, and flip through the book, or scan my code and visit the website to delve deeper into how it was made.
