Right off the bat, my ideas for this project were centred around storytelling, history, and 'copying' looked at from the perspective of the creative process. My ideas mostly fell under the categories of translation/mistanslation, reproduction and rituals/cultural traditions. I was also considering a huge range of possible physical outcomes and both analogue and digital techniques to get there. At this stage, I also had endless questions; what is the visual language? what is the story/content that I want to tell? how will it be graphic?

However, my understanding of a 'copy' is already quite clear here. I was also pretty clear on the outcome having some kind of digital and print elements, and that these would be based on a garment or textile. I thought back to my Image and Media project Rabid, which consisted of multiple costumes, three video projects and a printed magazine. I also thought about my Word as Image in Space and Time personal project Woman and Dog, which was a brand design work that included a physical garment, 3D modelling, website design, and printed packaging. This was the kind of scope that I believed I would be capable of delivering.
