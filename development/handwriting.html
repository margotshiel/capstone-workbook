I was looking to Petra Collins' Fairy Tales for inspiration on how to layout my book, and just could not find a typeface that I liked, and that I felt would work as well as the wide open script that book designer, Sandra Leko, had chosen. I thought about trying to use Grow, my personalised verison of Impact, but it was very obviously too heavy and basically unreadable in body text.

Then I went searching for script typefaces, which I generally dislike as a rule, and was unable to find one that I was happy with. So I decided to make my own, using my own handwriting. I wrote out the alphabet, numbers and punctuation and then scanned and cleaned these up. I added them to a calligraphr template and generated my own typeface, which I then used and LOVED in my book! This is also the typeface I have used for headings on my website.
