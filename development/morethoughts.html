Before my week 8 presentation, in week 7, I thought it would be beneficial to check in with myself and what direction I wanted to take my work in. I did this with a second mind map, and a draft of a research question, which you can see in my images. The question that I landed on was, "How does a combination of textile, digital and print help me find out about material and digital impermanence; the longevity of art, stories and people? What about immortality?" Here I was attempting to clarify the two versions of 'copying' that I am exploring in my project; process/printing/scanning/production, and the repetition of 'immortal' stories. The phrase that I kept repeating was, "Nothing lasts forever." I think this is both a pretty good representation of where my project was heading and what I wanted to say, but also maybe a manifestation of me feeling totally freaked out by the prospect of leaving university. I was also listening to the new Charli XCX album, Music, Fashion, Film, on repeat, which ends with a song titled No One Lasts Forever featuring David Cronenberg, which was probably stuck in my head.
