Suddenly, I had the realisation that, since I had made three dresses...I was going to need three mannequins to display them. I, myself, only have one and it is really quite ugly. My friend Josh has one that's nice looking, but it would not respond to the 1810s stays in the way that an actual body does, and so the dresses would look different on it. This was when I decided to cut my losses and solve the problem by deciding I do not need mannequins.

What I will do instead, is carefully undo the stitching on each of the pieces, and display them as fluttering fabric hangings instead. This will be a little tedious, but okay, because the fabric pieces were not cut at all during the process of sewing the gowns, I instead just draped and tacked them in place with minimal actual sewing.

make space!
