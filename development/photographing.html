I decided to photograph the dresses in a similar way to my Rabid Mag and other projects where I have had to act as director and model, but not camera man. In this case, my wonderful partner Aidan ventured into the mosquito filled garden with me at around 9pm and we used my little Canon camera with the flash on to capture the dresses in movement and in the darkness. I am really happy with how these came out. I honestly think that this technique for photographing my textiles work is pretty reliable at this point. I just always love how the images come out.
