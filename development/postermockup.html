The movie poster visualisation of your work was an activity from our week 6 PM class. I think this activity probably came a little early for me; I still had not done my first sun printing trials, or any actual physical production yet. As this physical processing is kind of the heart of my project, this timing made it hard to put anything together. I tried doing some digital manipulation of past fabric scans that I had, using bitmaping and saturation/hue adjustment layers, but I really don't think it was working at all. More than anything else, it does not look at all like a movie poster!
