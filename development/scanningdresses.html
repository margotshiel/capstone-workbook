I knew that I would need to document the printing on my dresses pretty quickly, because of the fugitive nature of the beetroot and turmeric dyes. For a dye to be fugitive, means that it is susceptible to fading from exposure to light, water and even air. This quality is what makes it possible to sun print with beetroot and turmeric in the first place. When exposed to UV, they fade very quickly, leaving behind marks where they have been covered. But it also means that they will not exist in their current form for very long at all.

I find this so so beautiful! This is a fleeting moment, and art does not have to last forever! We should be allowed to change with time and sunshine. This is another thing that makes these pieces so unique and irreplicable - they will not even remain as they are now for very long. Anyway! I booked the scanner at my local library and scanned my beautiful fabric at 1200 dpi.
