My experimentation with slashing was similar to the linocutting. In development of our outcome for the Post it to Prague brief in the AM session, I researched and tried a textile manipulation technique called slashing, where layers of fabric are stacked and glued to one another, then a pattern is stitched in and cuts made between the stitching lines. This exposes the layers of fabric underneath and adds depth, bulk and texture. Unfortunately, I basically just think this technique is ugly. Not only that, but by adding so much weight and stitching, you destroy drape in the fabric. I knew that to create any kind of flowy, layered or 1810s style gown, my fabric's drape would be very important to this experiment was a dead-end.
