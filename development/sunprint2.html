For my second trial with the anthotypes, made sure to let the prints expose for a long time, and in high UV. This, I think, was the thing that made the most difference in the clarity of the prints. They came out really beautifully clear in the end, with all of them super clearly legible. The fabric that I was testing was a georgette, and a chiffon, both of which were synthetic fibres.

Overall, the georgette came out much more nicely, and also retained a softer feeling after being treated. For the dying process, I applied multiple layers of both the beetroot and the turmeric, and boiled each other those dyes separately before applying them to the fabrics. The stencils I used this time around were blocky and heavy - I just freehand cut letters out a piece of card. The prints were left exposing in UV 4-8 sun over two days.
