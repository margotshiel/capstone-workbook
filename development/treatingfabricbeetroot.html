Treating fabric - beetroot

next

Now that I had a technique down, and fabric chosen for treating my prints, I bought six metres of georgette and split it into three sections. I covered my floor in plastic, laid down the fabric and began brushing it with the first layer of beetroot dye. I made the dye by draining and boiling the juice from a bunch of tinned beetroot (which was delicious). I painted two layers of the dye onto the fabric, letting it dry in between. The room had to be kept dark throughout this process, and I needed to treat one piece at a time.
