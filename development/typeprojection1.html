My plan B for creating a custom typeface was to project my story directly onto my skin, take a photo and then select the warped type out on photoshop. I tried this method, after failing to use a model of my body for the same purpose. The projection and photography steps went well, but when I got into photoshop, I found that the text I was using was too small to accurately select out, without doing it pixel by pixel. I hit a dead end with this, until a conversation with Willow helped me realise that I could instead create my own typeface. This would involve projecting each letter of the alphabet onto my skin, taking a photo and then selecting this warped letter out in photoshop. See the entry 'Type Projection 2' to see where I took this.
