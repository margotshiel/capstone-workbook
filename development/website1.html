I chatted with Willow, David and Narelle about the possibility of me being able to submit my visual journal in the form of a website, rather than a long pdf document. In the past, I have found that these documents just crash my computer, get me down, and are impossible to work on continuously throughout the semester, as my work develops. In semester 1, 2026, in Interactive Media, the same level of documentation was achieved in a constantly growing and evolving website called our workbook. I found the work flow on noting down my feelings, developments and thoughts in an excel sheet, and then transferring this onto a webpage was much more sustainable for me as I worked on a long term project. And that is how I came to produce the very website that you are reading from right now!

In the first stage of my web development, I decided to begin by working off the structure that I had established for my workbook in semester 1, 2026. This basically involved a homepage, glossary/blogroll, and a short page for each week of development. My initial styling was simply a matter of stripping out what I had worked into my other page, and restructing my site map into research, development and outcome.
