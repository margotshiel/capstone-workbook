In my week 11 presentation, I read out my final written statement for my project, and discussed my physical trials and progress to date. Here is my final written statement:

Grow is a new version of an immortal story. Using language, fabric and movement it tells the hero’s journey in three forms. Joseph Campbell synthesised coming of age stories across cultures and found that human narratives consistently follow a pattern : a call to action, the crossing of a threshold, and returning home changed. But this masculine understanding of storytelling ignores the equally transformative changes that occur within the body as we grow.

A garment, made from layers of sheer material, dyed with the sun, beetroot, turmeric and cyanotype, displays a new version of the hero’s journey, as she fights her sexuality and physicality. Grow then grew into the idea of processing itself. Printing, scanning, digital refinement, printing again and transforming the story for different formats. This led to the book and short film– transformations/reproductions/documentations of the garment.
