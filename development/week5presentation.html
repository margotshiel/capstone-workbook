Here is the written statement that I presented in week 5, and the accompanying images:

My capstone project will be an exploration into what is lost as gained as we tell and retell stories. It will use language, fabric and movement to tell of a hero’s journey in three forms. Joseph Campbell synthesised coming of age stories across cultures and found that human narratives consistently follow a pattern: a call to action, the crossing of a threshold, and returning home changed. While fascinating and useful, this interpretation flattens the nuance that makes each story unique. My project will be an attempt to honour these subtleties.

I love working with fabric. It’s basically all I do. And so, my proposal is that I produce a garment using sheer material, layering, and the application on type to compile a series of these stories into a single piece. The other area of research that I would like to investigate is the idea of processing itself. Printing, scanning, digital refinement, printing again and transforming my work for different formats. Which is why I plan to produce a book – a printed documentation of the fabric, that could be reproduced at scale. And short film – a loose, aesthetic drive and endlessly reproduceable digital work.
