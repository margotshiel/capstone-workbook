outcome

Grow is a new version of an immortal story. Using language, fabric and movement it tells the hero’s journey in three forms. Joseph Campbell synthesised coming of age stories across cultures and found that human narratives consistently follow a pattern : a call to action, the crossing of a threshold, and returning home changed. But this masculine understanding of storytelling ignores the equally transformative changes that occur within the body as we grow.

Three garments, made from layers of sheer material, dyed with the sun, beetroot, turmeric and cyanotype, display a new version of the hero’s journey. A version where she fights her sexuality and physicality. Grow then grew into the idea of processing itself. Printing, scanning, digital refinement, printing again and transforming the story for different formats. This led to the book and digital documentation of the work. The idea of copying or iterations being inherent to the artistic process, is displayed in my transformations / reproductions / documentations of the garment.
