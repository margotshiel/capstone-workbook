In my Image and Text Wayposts 1, I wrote about Anna Biller's work:

Both the colourful visual style of Biller’s cult classicThe Love Witch, and its satirical feminist content combine to amuse and encourage the viewer to question their own internalised misogyny. By maintaining a playful, almost childish view of what the perfect woman is, Biller’s protagonist Elaine subverts the very hyperfemininity and male fantasy that she seems to epitomise. In addition, Biller uses female bodily functions, such as menstruation and urination as motifs throughout her film, thus tapping into the genre of abject art.

I love this movie so so much, and I am waiting with baited breath to watch her next feature, The Face of Horror, which has jsut finished its initial festival run. Aside from the content and tone of the film, I also really love the quality of light and colour that the film uses. Their are moments of blinding raindow lights swirling, and shots of sun piercing darkest after passing through stained glass. Her website is also fantastic!
