Charles van Sandwyk is an illustrator and writer who brings to life simple, magical tales of the natural world and fabulous creatures. He works with pen, ink and water colour, as well as with etching, and his books are produced by him and his collaborator, Waisiki Doughty. They print on thick, hand made paper, using metallic inks and all of the books are hand bound. They are truly, truly beautiful and really fill me with joy.

Often using fairy tales and other old stories, van Sandwyk taps into something that I feel is deeply human and universal. He says, "We are servants of beauty." I began looking into van Sandwyk because I felt drawn to his illustrations - they reminded me of Enid Blyton and The Magic Faraway Tree (original illustrations by Dorothy M. Wheeler), but I quickly felt an affinity for this idea of ancient, simple and meaningful stories as well.
