Chiaroscuro is technique in painting, drawing and cinematography that uses high contrast lighting (ie. dark shadows and bright highlights) to create drama, tension and dimension in an artwork or moving image. Artemisia Gentileschi and other prominent Italian Renaissance painters are most famous for this technique, but I am especially fascinated by its use in photography. Artists like Petra Collins, Gregory Crewdson and Marion Drew, I think, all use a version of this technique to add feeling to their photographs.

I first learnt about light painting in photography (using a slow shutter speed and 'painting' the subject of the image with a torch) in 2022, and have been playing with it in my own work ever since. I really love the dreamy, surreal feeling that it gives to photos, and I like how it takes the edge off the 'reality' of an image. It invites the viewer to suspend their disbelief a little more.
