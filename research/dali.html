dali, surrealism and Dreaming

next

Salvador Dali's surrealist paintings are, of course, going to influence my exploration of dreams and unreality. His oil on canvas painting The Persistence of Memory,1931, is perhaps the most recognisable, but there is something about the 1946 Trilogy of the desert: Mirage, that brings me back to it time and time again. Maybe it is because I have been able to visit this piece in person so many times (it is housed at the NGV), or maybe it is because of the enchanting, haunting movement and body of the female figure. There is just something about the way that the fabric clings to her, and the line of the mirage leading back into the distance that makes me want to be inside the painting. I want to go visit that impossible, imagined city. There is also something poetic about the layers of unreality here; we are looking at a painting of a memory of a dream of a mirage.
