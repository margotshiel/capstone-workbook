Fleur Harris is an Australian artist and product designer who draws magical patterns and scenes of woodland creatures, flowers and foliage. Her work is really magical and she collaborates with Australian children's brands on packaging and clothing that is whimsical and enchanting. I first came across Harris' work at the toy shop I was working at in a collaboration she did with the children's 'makeup' brand Oh Flossy. The drawings immediately spoke to me and I think they relate well to the rest of my research into whimsical and beautiful drawings of fantastical creatures.
