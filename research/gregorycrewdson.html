In my Image and Text Wayposts 1, I wrote about Gregory Crewdson's work:

Crewdson’s use of lighting and staging is anything but crudely done. The careful curation of his sets creates an eerie, unsettling atmosphere, thus forcing the viewer to sit with their discomfort and consider what is causing it. Overall, his compositions are balanced and follow traditional photography conventions, and yet his stylistic choices and subject matter challenge and confront the viewer.

Please excuse the terrible pun, but I overall agree with what I said back in 2023. I also want to draw attention to his use of the natural enviroment, and motifs of decay, sadness and death in his work - shadows, rain, dead autumn leaves. I think it's really beautiful how these elements often find their way into the interior/domestic spaces that he photographs. It is very unsettling.
