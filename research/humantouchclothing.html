Human Touch Clothing is a fashion brand, and creator of the technique paint-sewing. As their website states, "Today and for the foreseeable future, the making of every piece of clothing worldwide will involve sewing by human hands." Sewing is only of the only production processes that has not been completely industrialised, and has remained basically unchanged for a century. What has changed is who makes our clothing and where.

By covering their hands in paint before handling a garment, tailors and seamstresses make their physical labor visible. It has always been there, but in the Western world, we really do not understand the level of labor that goes into making our clothing - labor that is often completed under dangerous conditions, or for little to no pay. What Human Touch is doing, is making this absolutely visible, which I think is not only admirable, but also important.
