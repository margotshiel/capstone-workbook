Joseph Campbell was an American writer and professor who worked in comparative mythology and religion. One of his most famous works, The Hero with a Thousand Faces,(1949) will form the basis of my project. In its most simplified form, this book highlights connections in the way that different cultures tells stories. It suggests that there is an ancient and universal story of an archetypal hero who leaves home and returns changed.

I love this idea of connection and unity throughout humanity, and the thing that connects us being the shape of the stories that we tell to make sense of our own lives. This shape is most commonly represented as a circle, the hero's journey that follows nine stages, through which some kind of truth or transformation is revealed. I am using this concept of a repeated, retold, universal story, or "monomyth" as Campbell calls it, as the core 'copy' of my project.
