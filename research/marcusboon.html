For Marcus Boon's book, In Praise of Copying, I just read the introdution. I love the cheeky opening about Disneyland's Space Mountain and kind of want to go to Disneyland now and meditate on all of the rides. I also like the way his musings on copying immediately spiral to the ultimate question of, isn't everything a copy? I do kind of feel that this is true - we are all a product of our environments, and this very research section that I am currently writing in is a testament to that. My creative decisions are in many ways informed by all of the work I have seen other people doing.
