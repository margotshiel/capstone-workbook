The Odyssey, is an ancient epic poem, that tells the story of Odysseus as he journeys to return home to Ithica, after the events of the Trojan War. Traditionally attributed to the poet Homer, it is impossible to know the exact origin of the poem, which was written around the 8th century BCE. The story likely emerged from a long oral tradition of poets and was some combination of many people's work. This may have been put to paper buy a single person, but we truly can never know. Over the course of the poem, which has been translated countless times, (I have read Emily Wilson's 2017 translation) Odysseus is changed by what he goes through, before returning to the island where he is King.

He is a changed man, and this is at the heart of all 'hero's journey' stories. This story itself has also be retold and reimagined over the course of thousands of years, most recently by Margaret Atwood, in The Penelopiad, and Christopher Nolan in his 2026, star-studded IMax Epic, The Odyssey.
