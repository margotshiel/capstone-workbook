The beautiful stories of Snugglepot and Cuddlepie are children's books written and illustrated by May Gibbs in 1918. The stories are about a pair of gumnut babies (tiny babies who live inside of gumnuts, of course) and they were among my favourites as a child. There is something so quintessentially Australian about Snugglepot and Cuddlepie, and to me they work as a kind of Australian version of the drawings of Cicely Mary Brown. I have included this reference, just as another illustration of the kind of wonder and magic that I want to try an capture.
